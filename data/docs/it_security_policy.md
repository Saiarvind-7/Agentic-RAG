# IT Security Policy (POL-305)

## Accounts and passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is
required for email, the code repository and the cloud console. Never share a password,
even with IT staff.

## Devices
All company laptops use full-disk encryption. If a laptop or phone is lost or stolen,
report it within 2 hours to security@northwind.example so the device can be wiped remotely.
On public Wi-Fi, always connect through the company VPN.

## Phishing
If you receive a suspicious email, do not click anything. Use the Report Phish button in
your mail client. If you already clicked a link, tell the security team right away.

## Training and reviews
Every employee finishes the security awareness course by October 31 each year.
System owners review who has access to their systems every quarter.

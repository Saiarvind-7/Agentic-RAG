# Leave Policy (POL-117)

Northwind Labs, Bangalore. Applies to all full-time employees.

## Annual leave
Every employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave builds up at
2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried over to the next year, and they
must be used by March 31. Anything beyond 10 days is lost at the end of December.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is needed once
sick leave goes beyond 3 days in a row.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver gets 26 weeks of paid leave. The secondary caregiver gets 6 weeks.

## How to apply
Submit the request in the HR portal. For absences longer than 2 days, apply at least
5 working days ahead. Your manager approves or declines within 2 working days.

# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy (POL-204)

Northwind Labs works in a hybrid model.

## Office days
Employees come to the office at least 3 days a week. Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are
the core days when most teams meet in person. Core working hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST,
wherever you are working from.

## Working from another city
With your manager's approval you can work from a different city for up to 20 days in a
calendar year. Requests go through the HR portal at least 10 working days before you travel.

## Home office support
New joiners get a one-time home office stipend of 20,000 INR for a desk, chair or monitor.
The company also reimburses internet costs up to 1,000 INR per month against a bill.
